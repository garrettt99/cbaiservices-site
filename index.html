Questions

Straight answers.

Will this replace my staff?

No. It handles the repetitive work and response gaps: answering routine questions, capturing lead details, following up and helping with booking. Your team stays in control of the customer relationship and anything that needs human judgment.

What does it cost?

Focused systems start at $697 setup plus $147/month. The AI Receptionist is $997 setup plus $197/month, and the Complete System Bundle is $1,500 setup plus $297/month. Your free consultation confirms the right starting point before anything is priced.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. These systems work with your existing business number. If you'd rather keep automation on a separate line, we can set that up too.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

It answers from information we set up together, like your services, area and process, and hands off to a person when a question goes beyond that. You can review every conversation, so anything off gets caught and corrected quickly.

How long does setup take?

A single focused system is usually up and running within a couple of weeks of your consultation. You get a clear timeline before any work starts.

Do I need to change my current tools?

Not necessarily. We start with the tools you already use, then find the smallest practical improvement and confirm what connects before recommending a build.
