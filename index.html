Common questions before getting started.

Clear answers about fit, setup, and what a first conversation covers.

Will this replace my staff?

The focus is repetitive work and response gaps: answering routine questions, capturing lead details, sending follow-up, and helping with booking. Your team stays in control of the customer relationship and the work that needs human judgment.

Do I need to change all of my current tools?

Not necessarily. The first step is understanding the tools and process you already use. We then identify the smallest practical improvement and confirm what can connect before recommending a build.

Can I hear the AI receptionist before deciding?

Yes. The recorded live receptionist demo is available on this site, and the free consultation can focus on how a similar call flow would fit your business.

What happens in the free consultation?

We discuss where leads or admin work get stuck, what a better process should look like, and which service—if any—is the right starting point. There is no need to prepare technical information.

What does it cost?

Packages start with focused systems — for example, the AI Receptionist is $997 setup plus $197/month, and the Follow-Up & Review Engine is $697 setup plus $147/month. Every engagement starts with a free consultation where we confirm scope and the right starting point before anything is priced.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

The AI answers from information we set up together — your services, area, and process — and hands off to a human when a question goes beyond that. You can review every conversation, so anything off gets caught and corrected quickly.

How long does setup take?

A single focused system is typically up and running within a couple of weeks of the consultation. You’ll get a clear timeline for your setup before any work starts — no open-ended projects.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. These systems work with your existing business number. If you’d rather keep automation on a separate line, we can set that up too.
