Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026. Plain-language summary of what we collect and how we use it.

Cape Breton AI Services (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

Contact details you give us: name, email address, phone number, business name, and anything you type into our forms, chat, or lead ads (for example, what kind of business you have and your biggest time-waster).

If you contact us through a Facebook lead form, we receive the answers you submit there.

Basic website analytics, such as which pages you visit, to help us improve the site.

How we use it

To respond to your inquiries and provide the services you ask about, such as booking your free 15-minute demo.

To follow up with you by phone, text, or email about our services.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Sharing and storage

Your information is stored securely and shared only with the tools we need to run the business (for example, our website host, booking calendar, and messaging tools).

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your personal information at any time — just contact us below.
