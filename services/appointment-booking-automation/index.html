THE PROBLEM

Scheduling eats time and loses momentum.

Every round of phone tag is a chance for the customer to drift away or book elsewhere. Manual calendar updates also create double-bookings and gaps that cost you working hours.

THE SOLUTION

A booking flow customers can complete themselves.

We set up online booking tied to your real availability: the customer picks a time that works, gets an instant confirmation, and receives reminders before the appointment. Your calendar stays accurate without manual updates.

THE BENEFIT

More booked appointments, less admin.

Customers book when it suits them — including evenings — and your team spends less of the day coordinating schedules.

Common questions.

What if I need to approve bookings first?

We can set it either way — instant booking for standard appointments, or request-and-approve where you want control.

Will it double-book me?

No. It reads your real availability and blocks times as they are taken, with buffers you define.

Can customers reschedule themselves?

Yes — a simple reschedule link in the confirmation keeps changes from becoming phone tag.

Does it work with my current calendar?

We connect it to the calendar setup you already use wherever possible.
