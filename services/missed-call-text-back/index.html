THE PROBLEM

A missed caller often calls the next business.

When nobody picks up and no text follows, most callers keep dialing down the list. The job goes to whoever responds first — and that is rarely the business that was busy doing the work.

THE SOLUTION

An immediate, helpful text the moment a call is missed.

The caller receives a friendly text acknowledging them within seconds of the missed call. It opens a conversation, asks what they need, and keeps them engaged until you or your team can respond properly.

THE BENEFIT

Missed calls stop being missed opportunities.

Callers feel acknowledged instead of ignored, and you get the conversation started automatically — even in the middle of a job.
